Handy

Touch anywhere on the video to pause.

Select the video and its .srt file together. Two .srt files (for example German and Turkish) work too. Nothing is uploaded: your files stay on this device.

Full screen on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Handy from your home screen.

    Pick the same video again and your place and subtitles come back.

    How to use
    • Touch anywhere on the video to pause. Tap again to play.
    • While paused you get the seek bar, −5 s / +5 s, speed and subtitles (CC).
    • CC shows one subtitle file, both at once, or none.
    • Your place and subtitles are remembered for each video.
    • On iPhone, keep the videos and .srt files in an iCloud Drive folder so they show up in the Files app.
    • Computer keys: Space pause/play, ← → 5 s, S subtitles, , . speed, F full screen.
    Videos that won't play

    iPhone plays MP4 videos in H.264 or HEVC. MKV and WebM files, and AV1 or VP9 videos (common in YouTube downloads), may not play.

    Download in H.264 with yt-dlp:

    yt-dlp -S "vcodec:h264,res:1080,acodec:m4a" --merge-output-format mp4 URL

    Or convert a video on a Mac with ffmpeg:

    ffmpeg -i input.mkv -c:v h264_videotoolbox -b:v 6M -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 160k -sn -movflags +faststart output.mp4
